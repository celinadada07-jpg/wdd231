Naija Kitchen

Nigerian Food & Recipes

1. Site Name

Naija Kitchen

The name "Naija Kitchen" was selected because "Naija" is a familiar informal name for Nigeria, while "Kitchen" clearly communicates the focus of the website. The name is simple, memorable, and connected to Nigerian food and cooking.

Possible domain: naijakitchen.com

2. Site Purpose

Naija Kitchen provides a collection of Nigerian food recipes. The website will help visitors discover popular Nigerian dishes, learn about the ingredients required, and follow step-by-step cooking instructions.

Visitors will also be able to explore different Nigerian meals by category and learn more about Nigerian food and cooking traditions.

3. Scenarios

  • How do I prepare a traditional Nigerian jollof rice?
  • What ingredients do I need to prepare egusi soup?
  • What Nigerian meals can I make for a family gathering?
  • Where can I find easy Nigerian recipes for beginners?

4. Color Scheme

The website will use a warm and natural color scheme inspired by Nigerian food, fresh ingredients, and Nigerian culture.

Deep Green — #006B3C

Deep green will be used for the header, navigation, headings, buttons, and important accents.

Warm Gold — #F2B705

Warm gold will be used for accents, highlights, borders, buttons, and interactive elements.

Cream — #FFF8E7

Cream will be used as the main page background to create a warm and welcoming appearance.

5. Typography

Poppins

Poppins will be used for headings, navigation text, page titles, and important section headings. It provides a modern and clear appearance.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, recipe descriptions, ingredient lists, and general body text because it is easy to read.
