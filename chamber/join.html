NP Membership

NP Membership is designed for nonprofit organizations.

  • No membership fee
  • Access to chamber networking events
  • Community business resources
  • Training opportunities

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides basic benefits for growing businesses.

  • Business networking opportunities
  • Access to selected training events
  • Member discounts
  • Business resources

Silver Membership

Silver Membership provides additional opportunities for business growth and visibility.

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Additional training opportunities
  • Event discounts
  • Business promotion opportunities
  • Special networking events

Gold Membership

Gold Membership provides premium benefits and increased business visibility.

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Premium advertising opportunities
  • Home page spotlight opportunities
  • Priority event opportunities
  • Premium networking opportunities
  • Additional promotional benefits
